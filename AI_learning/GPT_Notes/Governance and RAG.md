- What information should enter the knowledge system?
- What constitutes an authoritative source?
- How do we represent relationships between business concepts and physical data?
- How do we preserve provenance?
- How do we deal with conflicting sources?
- How do we detect stale knowledge?
- How do we establish permissions?
- How do we distinguish an authoritative fact from someone's opinion?
- How do we determine what context an agent needs?
- How do we evaluate whether retrieval is actually correct?
- How do we govern changes to the knowledge layer?
- How does an agent know **which knowledge source to trust**?

Those are much closer to **data management / metadata / knowledge architecture** than traditional RAG engineering.

RAG infrastructure is becoming a commodity. Knowledge engineering is not.
